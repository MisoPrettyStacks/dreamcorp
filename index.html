Questions

Is this real or a demo?

This page is an interactive demo. The agents, revenue, and activity are simulated locally in your browser — nothing leaves your device.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe the business in plain language; the agents handle design, building, and operations.

What does it cost?

Starting is free. This demo costs nothing and needs no account.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Your company, stats, and chat history are stored only in your browser’s local storage.

Can I reset the demo?

Yes — open your company dashboard and use the “Reset demo” button to start over.
