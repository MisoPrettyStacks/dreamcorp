Questions

What do I actually get?

A public website (repo + live URL), a backend app with a database, a working inbox, your own admin login, a step-by-step tutorial, and an agent workforce that runs daily briefings and executes your directives.

How do the agents work?

On a schedule, your workforce researches, briefs, and maintains your company — every run is logged in Agent Activity. Directives you give in chat are executed for real. Your autonomy level decides how much happens without asking you first.

What about a real email address?

You get a working inbox inside your backend from day one. A real you@company address needs a domain plus a short guided setup — choose it in the interview and we prepare everything.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe the business in plain language and pick from real options; the workforce handles the rest.

What does it cost?

Starting is free — the whole stack runs on free tiers. No credit card required.

Where is my data?

Your companies live in real repos and backends owned by you. Only your unfinished wizard draft is kept in this browser’s local storage.
